The Raven (From what I can remember)

Once upon a midnight dreary,
While I pondered weak and weary
Over a many quaint and curious volume of forgotten lore,
While I nodded, nearly napping,
Suddenly, there came a tapping,
As of someone gently rapping, rapping at my chamber door.
"Tis some visitor", I muttered, "tapping at my chamber door,
Only this, and nothing more."

Ah, distinctly I remember,
It was in the bleak december,
And each seperate dying ember wrought its ghost upon the floor.
Eagerly I wished the morrow,
Vainly I had sought to borrow
From my books secrease of sorrow,
Sorrow for the lost Lenore,
For the rare and radiant maiden which the angels named Lenore,
nameless here forevermore.

And the silken sad unscertain
rustling of each purple curtain
Thrilled me, filled me with fantastic terrors,
So that now to still the beating of my heart I stood repeating:
"Tis some visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door,
Some late night visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door,
Only this and nothing more."
